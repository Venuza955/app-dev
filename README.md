# My Favorite Series: Black Clover

**Black Clover** is a Japanese fantasy anime series based on the manga written and illustrated by *Yūki Tabata*.

## Why I Recommend It
* **Underdog Story:** Asta is born without magic in a world where magic is everything, yet he never gives up on his dream to become the Wizard King.
* **Epic Battles:** The teamwork and fight choreography with the Black Bulls squad are intense and hype.
* **Character Growth:** Every member of the Black Bulls overcomes their past flaws and grows stronger together.

> "My magic is never giving up!" — Asta

### Main Characters
1. Asta
2. Yuno Grinberryall
3. Noelle Silva
4. Yami Sukehiro
